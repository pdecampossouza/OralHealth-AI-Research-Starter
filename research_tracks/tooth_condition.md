# Tooth visual-condition classification

A future project may classify tooth-level visual conditions after reliable detection/segmentation. Terms such as `healthy`, `altered`, `trauma`, or `caries` require a dental-expert-defined ontology and verified labels. The starter kit intentionally does not derive these labels from appearance or filenames.

A suitable project should separate the localization problem from the condition-classification problem and quantify propagation of localization errors into classification.
