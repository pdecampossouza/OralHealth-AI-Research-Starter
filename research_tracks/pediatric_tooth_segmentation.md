# Pediatric tooth instance segmentation

## Research opportunity
How do primary and mixed dentition affect robustness and transferability of tooth instance-segmentation models?

## Starting point
Use the pediatric sample for pipeline verification and retrieve the complete pediatric collections for research experiments.

## What the kit does not solve
It does not classify primary versus permanent teeth, infer eruption status, or label a visible space as a missing tooth.

## Questions to investigate
Evaluate transfer from adult-trained representations, fine-tuning strategies, partially erupted teeth, spacing, instance separation, and human-in-the-loop annotation efficiency. In pediatric images, spacing is **not automatically evidence of a missing tooth**.
