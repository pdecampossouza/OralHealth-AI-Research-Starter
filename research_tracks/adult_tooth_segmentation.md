# Adult tooth instance segmentation

## Research opportunity
How accurately can pretrained instance-segmentation architectures isolate individual teeth in heterogeneous intraoral photographs?

## Starting point
Use the adult sample, subject-level split utilities, classical baselines, generic Mask R-CNN demonstration, and gallery scaffold.

## What the kit does not solve
It does not select the best dental architecture, provide complete tooth masks, fine-tune a dental model, or define missing-tooth logic.

## Questions to investigate
Compare architectures, study adjacent-tooth merging/splitting, evaluate contour quality, quantify robustness to view/image quality, and validate representative outputs with dental experts.
